Greens for the colder half of the year

Summer had the tomato and the cucumber; spring and autumn had one lettuce. Three more leaf crops now fill the shoulder seasons — and one of them, kale, is the first plant in the mod that a winter does not kill. Lettuce is redrawn to stand beside them.

One

Three greens for spring and autumn

All three are sown on farmland from their own seeds and grow through eight stages, the way every farmland crop here does. What separates them is what a harvest costs you: cabbage is taken whole, and kale and spinach can be picked without tearing the plant up — right-click a ripe one and it drops back a few stages and carries on, so a bed sown once feeds you for the rest of its season.

Two

Kale does not die in winter

Until now a plant met winter one of two ways. A crop in a field died — replaced where it stood, resown if the world had planted it, turned to dead growth if you had. A bush sat the season out by swapping itself for a dormant one. Kale does neither. It is left exactly where it is, at whatever stage it had reached, and carries on in spring.

It is still out of season, and that is the whole price of standing there: it will not grow, a warm compost bed underneath will not push it along, and there is nothing to pick until the thaw. What changes is only that winter no longer costs you the bed. Sow kale in autumn, let it reach whatever stage it reaches, and it is still your kale in spring. A village kale bed is left standing for the same reason, rather than being torn up and sown again.

A frozen plant is not a growing one. Surviving winter is its own block tag, rpg4fools:survives_winter, and not a fifth season to grow in — a crop that grew in winter could also be sown in winter and would ripen through it. Kale can do neither. A datapack can put anything it likes on that tag; kale is simply the first plant drawn for it.

Three

Lettuce, redrawn

Lettuce was the only leaf crop in the mod, and it had been drawn as one. With three more beside it, it was suddenly the odd one out — a different hand, next to plants that share a shape and a palette. All eight of its stages and its item sprite are redrawn so a mixed bed reads as one set of greens rather than as four separate mods.

Nothing else about it moved. Same seasons, same food, same harvest: the plant you have in the ground is the plant you had. The 0.3.1 page still shows the art it is replacing, since a released page keeps the sprites that shipped with it.

Also

In this release

Villages grow them

A farm generates with whatever is in season, so cabbage, kale and spinach turn up in village beds from spring and autumn onward — and a kale bed found in winter is one that was there in autumn.

Nothing to migrate

Three new crops and three new seeds, added alongside what you already have. An existing world keeps its fields exactly as they are; the new plants appear where the world generates fresh.

A crop is now two kinds of thing

Internally, a farmland crop and a bush are separate types rather than one record with the other's fields left blank. Nothing about how the mod plays changes with it — a bush simply can no longer claim a trellis, and a crop can no longer claim thorns.

Where the seeds come from

Breaking a plant drops its seeds, as every crop here does, and all three are in the mod's creative tab. A village farm is the other way in — the beds are sown with the seasons.
