You can see when it is ready

An art release. Lettuce is drawn as a lettuce instead of a green blob, tomato and cucumber flower before they fruit so a ripe plant is obvious from across the field, and the hidden flag they needed to look right has been taken out.

One

Lettuce is a lettuce

The old sprite was a symmetrical green mound with no leaf structure, which read as a shrub in a row of shrubs. All eight stages are redrawn as loose-leaf lettuce: frilly lobes, a jagged rim and no head, opening from two seed leaves out to a rosette that reaches the edge of the block. Nothing about how it grows or what it drops has changed.

Two

Flowers first, then fruit

Tomato and cucumber fruit more than once, and telling a ripe plant from an almost-ripe one used to mean counting pixels. Cucumber was the worst of it: the fruit was painted within ten RGB units of the leaves around it, so ripe and not-ripe differed by twelve pixels of near-identical green. Both plants now spend their last three stages saying what they are doing.

Stage 4 · bare

The plant is full grown and carrying nothing. This is also where it lands after you pick it.

Stage 5 · one flower

A yellow blossom opens. Nothing to take yet, but the plant is working.

Stage 6 · two flowers

A second blossom. Still nothing to take.

Stage 7 · fruit

The flowers are gone and fruit hangs where they were. The only stage you can pick.

Three

One less thing to remember

Both plants used to carry a picked boolean on the block, for one reason: so a picked plant could be drawn bare instead of half grown. It doubled each blockstate to sixteen entries and needed a stage-4 sprite that no age could reach on its own. Now that stage 4 is the bare plant, the flag has nothing left to say and has been removed.

Picking works the same

Right-click a ripe plant, take one to three of the produce, and it drops back to stage 4 to flower again. Only the bookkeeping changed.

Half the blockstate

Sixteen variants down to eight on both crops, one model per age, no property to match against.

Two files gone

The stage4_picked texture and model for each crop are deleted. Their art lives on as the new stage 4.

A smoother run to adult

Stage 3 inherits the plant that used to be stage 4 on both crops, so reaching the adult is a step rather than a jump.

Existing worlds are fine

A planted crop keeps its age. A plant that was carrying the old flag simply stops carrying it and draws as the stage it is on.

Nothing else moved

Seasons, loot tables, harvest behaviour and the seed items are all untouched. This release is sprites and the state they made unnecessary.
