0.1.0 · Released 19 Jul 2024

A calendar, and somewhere to read it

The first release. A world gets a date, the date gets a season, and the season gets a corner of the screen.

What it added

The season on your HUD

One icon per season

Shown in the corner of the HUD
spring
summer
autumn
winter
halloween
christmas
Halloween and Christmas take over the icon on their own dates, ahead of whichever season they fall in.

Months mapped to seasons

A world keeps a day count, the day count gives a month, and the month gives the season everything else reads.

Kept per world

The date is saved with the world and sent to the client, so it survives a restart and every player agrees on it.
