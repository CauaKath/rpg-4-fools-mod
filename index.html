Fabric mod · Minecraft 1.20.6

RPG 4 Fools

Seasons that the world actually obeys: the ground changes colour, the weather turns, and a crop only grows in the months that suit it. Release notes, one page a version.

Release notes

Seven versions so far

  1. 0.4.1 A quieter turn of the day The world no longer redraws itself at dawn, the season tint is worked out once a day instead of once per block, and the date is sent when it changes rather than twenty times a second. Released1 Sep 2026
  2. 0.4.0 A field worth building on A trellis a tomato climbs, a wall a cucumber spreads across, compost worked into the soil three ways, and a hoe that harvests the square around the crop it clicked. Released1 Sep 2026
  3. 0.3.1 You can see when it is ready Lettuce redrawn as a lettuce, tomato and cucumber flower before they fruit, and the picked flag they needed is gone. Released30 Aug 2026
  4. 0.3.0 Seasons decide what grows Six new plants with their own calendar, wild berry bushes, right click harvest, and fields that sow themselves again. Released30 Aug 2026
  5. 0.2.0 The world takes on the season Seasonal grass, foliage, sky and fog, winter snowfall that melts again, and sub-seasons so the change arrives gradually. Released28 Aug 2026
  6. 0.1.1 The overlay knows when to leave The season overlay is hidden in creative and spectator. Released19 Jul 2024
  7. 0.1.0 A calendar, and somewhere to read it Months mapped to seasons, kept per world and shown on the HUD, with holidays of their own. Released19 Jul 2024
