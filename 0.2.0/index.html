0.2.0 · Released 28 Aug 2026

The world takes on the season

Seasons stop being a number on the HUD and start showing up in the world itself: in the colour of the grass, in the sky, in what falls out of it.

What changed

Colour, weather and snow

Grass and foliage retinted

Every biome's grass and leaf colour is shifted towards the season, so the ground reads as spring or autumn before you check anything.

Sky and fog follow

Sky colour, fog colour and fog distance are all seasonal, with caves and weather kept on their own rules so neither is washed out by the season above ground.

Winter falls as snow

Rain becomes snowfall in biomes that would normally be too warm for it, and the snow that settles melts again as the year warms up.

Sub-seasons

Each season is split into parts, so the world slides from one to the next over days rather than changing colour at a stroke on the first morning.

The season is the server's

The date is kept in world data and pushed to every client, so what you see and what the world does can never drift apart.

Releases build themselves

Bumping the mod version publishes a tagged release with the built jar attached, instead of a rolling development build nobody could cite.
